## 00 systems

The brief was one small building in five material systems, drawn to be built. On paper
the systems stack: *concrete holds the ground, brick makes the room, wood makes the floor,
metal ties the parts, bamboo carries the roof.* The exploded drawing made that reading
easy. It also hid the whole problem: **five materials meet in ten possible pairs, and the
drawing had nothing to say about any of the meetings.** [confirm]

## 01 tools

The studio began on the bench, not the site. Tools counted, bamboo cut to a common
length, steel sized on the chop saw. The first thing a one-to-one studio teaches is how
long everything takes; the second is that every material arrives with its own tolerances.
[confirm]

## 02 ground

Concrete first: a trench under the trees, rebar in it, the first bricks bedded after dark.
The first meeting was concrete to brick at the footing, and it set the line every course
above would have to keep. [confirm]

## 03 raise

The walls went up inside a scaffold a course at a time. The bamboo roof frame was made
flat on the ground and carried in whole, over our heads, by everyone at once. The drawing
had shown it arriving; it had not said how, and it had not drawn the wall head where brick
would take the bamboo. [confirm]

## 04 joint

Where the drawing ran out. Bamboo does not take a bolt cleanly and does not sit flat on
brick; it wanted lashing, packing, a tolerance the plan had not allowed. **The detail was
resolved on the roof, by hand, and drawn afterwards** so the next group would know.
[confirm]
