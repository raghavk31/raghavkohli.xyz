## 00 the city

Surat is India's textile and diamond city and one of its fastest-growing — eight million
people in the municipal limits, a river that floods, a coast at its back. This plan
starts, as the Ahmedabad and Vadodara plans did, from one municipality in one year:
Surat, 2023–24, by zone and by ward, in the units the corporation uses.

## 01 the atlas

Before the plan proposes anything, it draws. Twenty-two maps at ward level, in three
passes. First the **infrastructure** as it stands: wastewater, drainage, waste,
transport, green cover, parks, fire cover, health, air. Then the **hazards**: the
slums and schools inside the heat, the flood-prone areas and the disease complaints
that follow them. Then the cross: heat laid over water supply, flood over wastewater,
flood over waste, flood over the roads and the emergency services that depend on them —
and, last, one composite of every hazard against every fragile system.

## 02 the inventory

The maps say where; the inventory says how much. Prepared to the Global Protocol for
Community-Scale emissions at the BASIC level, with a five-year series behind it, the sum
is a number the city can be held to: **10.43 million tonnes of CO₂e in 2023–24**, 1.32
tonnes per person — manufacturing 40%, transport 26%, homes 20%. Energy use is the
inverse: transport first, at 46%.

Five years of the same accounting give the trend: 26.9% up, 6.7% a year. That is the
business-as-usual line the rest of the plan argues against.

## 03 the pathway

Surat chose the harder target. Where the national commitment is net zero by 2070, the
city's pathway is drawn to **2047**, India's centenary year. Three lines: business
as usual, rising to 36.8 million tonnes; the progressive scenario, every existing scheme
pushed as far as it goes; and the net-zero pathway, which extends each action until the
remaining line meets the target — 46.3% below BAU in 2030, 54.8% in 2035, 74.3% in 2047.

Buildings do most of the work again: 75% of fans and 90% of lighting efficient, half the
energy demand from renewables, 80% of new buildings green by 2047. Transport shifts 30% of
private trips to walking, cycling and public transport, and electrifies the bus fleet.

## 04 strategies

Nine sectors this time — health joins the eight — each with one goal, a handful of
strategies, and targets by decade. My part of the book begins here: the strategies were
drawn before they were tabulated, so that a department head could see the piece of city
each one changes, and the whole set fits on one sheet.

## 05 actions

The action sheets are the part of the plan that gets picked up. Each sector opens with
its 2023–24 baseline and the gaps the audit found; each action follows with its target,
the entity that implements it, the financing mode, the indicative cost and timeline, and
a resilience score. A sheet is what a department takes to a budget meeting.
