Five months embedded across twelve fishing communities in the Koliwadas of
Mumbai — participatory, ground-level, on their terms. The thesis won CEPT's
**Student Excellence Award** ("Living Heritage"). This is where I learned that
making a system legible means starting inside it, not above it.
