Rooftop solar turns a household into a producer, but the grid still treats it as
a consumer with an awkward meter. **Sama asks what changes when a prosumer can
see their own energy as a tradeable thing** — priced, visible, exchangeable with
a neighbour. Built on the India Energy Stack and the Beckn Protocol, with a
working Python BPP module at its core.
