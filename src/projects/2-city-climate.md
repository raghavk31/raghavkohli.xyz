Five years of GHG inventories, climate-risk assessments, and action plans across
15+ cities — strategies carrying **8M+ tCO₂e of reduction potential** and **$25B+
of identified investment**. The plans were only ever as good as the data
underneath, and the data layer kept failing in the same ways. This is where the
obsession started.
