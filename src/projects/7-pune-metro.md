## 00 the line

Line 3 runs from Megapolis Circle in Hinjewadi through Balewadi and Baner to Shivajinagar,
23.33 kilometres on elevated track, twenty-three stations roughly a kilometre apart. Lines 1 and 2
are Maha Metro's; Line 3 is a public–private partnership — Tata Realty and Siemens Project
Ventures in a consortium with PMRDA, an SPV called Pune IT City Metro Rail that designs, builds,
finances and operates the line for thirty-three years and then hands it over. The contract is
why the stations come with land: the concession is paid for partly in commercial development.

## 01 three models

Along the line the commercial space takes three forms. Below the station, in the volume the
viaduct leaves under the platform. At mezzanine level, inside the station box between the
paid and unpaid areas. And on vacant land beside the station, leased out by PMRDA. The three
diagrams set the terms for everything after: **the station is a building, a landlord and a
landowner at once**, and the design has to hold all three.

## 02 massing

PMR-12 at Ram Nagar is the third case: an 11.74-acre plot, a quarter in front on the road and
three-quarters behind. The study moves the mandatory 5 per cent amenity into the front parcel so
it joins the public plaza at the station's foot, and then tests the massing — four two-storey
options for the front, and for the back the same 1.85 lakh square metres at seventy-five per cent
cover and seven floors, fifty and ten, or forty and thirteen. Arithmetic a developer and a
municipality can argue over, drawn so the argument is about the ground.

## 03 layout 01

The typical station, level by level. At road level, entries from both footpaths, bus stops and
drop-offs on each side, cycle and two-wheeler parking, the vertical cores, and a service lift for
the tenant below. At mezzanine, the paid-to-paid and unpaid connections either side of 2,956
square metres of public development. At platform, the paid area on the platforms, breakout spaces
and back-of-house for station and crew at the ends. **Every square metre is tagged paid, unpaid or
public** — the drawing is a lease as much as a plan.

## 04 layout 02

The second layout is the same station on a different road, between NICMAR, the PMPML bus depot
and Balewadi High Street: entries and exits on both sides, a DG room, the ticket hall and 700
square metres of retail at mezzanine, the equipment rooms for signalling and communications,
kiosks and outdoor seating on the concourse, solar on the roof.

## 05 section

The type is a cantilever: concourse over mezzanine over road, the roof cantilevered out past the
platforms so the street below stays open. Standing-seam aluminium roofing, mild-steel vertical
louvres, skylights over the concourse, high-performance glass, cast in-situ epoxy floors, tactile
paving for the blind. The isometric names every material. It was the last drawing of the
internship, and the first time a building I had drawn was measured in tender quantities.
