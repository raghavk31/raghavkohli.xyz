## 00 the line

Line 3 of the Pune Metro is a public–private line from Hinjewadi to Shivajinagar, elevated the
whole way through the corridor where the city's IT parks are. CCBA Designs was designing its
stations. Each station lands with a parcel of land around it, and at PMR-12, Ram Nagar, that
parcel is 11.74 acres at a junction — a quarter of it in front, on the road, and three-quarters
behind.

The office's drawings were the stations; the internship's were the ground. A base drawing of
the station box over the road, the plot line, and the plaza edge to be designed.

## 01 the ground

Three diagrams set the terms. The amenity parcel: the corner of the site nearest the junction,
where a bus stop, a stair and a shopfront all want to be. The radius: what is within a short
walk of the platform, drawn onto the parcel so that whatever goes there is reached on foot. The
plaza: the piece of the front parcel given to the street rather than to the building — **the
station's foot, made a place instead of a gap under a viaduct.**

## 02 front parcel

The front parcel is 2.93 acres. Four options were drawn, all at two storeys, between twenty and
thirty per cent land cover, from 4,542 to 6,842 square metres of floor area. What changes between
them is not the quantity but the shape of the ground left over: whether the plaza reads as a
forecourt, a passage or a corner, and how the block stands to the junction.

## 03 back parcel

The back parcel is 8.80 acres and carries the development that pays for the station. The three
options hold the floor area constant — a little under 1.85 lakh square metres — and trade ground
for height: seventy-five per cent cover at seven floors, fifty at ten, forty at thirteen. The
diagrams are deliberately mute about architecture. They are the arithmetic a developer and a
municipality can argue over, drawn so that the argument is about the ground.

## 04 the station

For the record, this is what the office was building: an elevated concourse with a louvred
timber screen, a plaza under it, stairs and a lift to the street. The massing study sits around
stations like this one. It is included because the study makes no sense without it, and credited
because it is not mine.
