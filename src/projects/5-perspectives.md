## 02 the hypothesis

The project started as a bet on format. Write the protocol well enough and anyone can run it.
Hand it to enough people and depth becomes a property of the system rather than of whoever is
hosting.

What came back moved the weight off the protocol and onto the person running it. The protocol
held from room to room. The rooms did not.

## 12 lineage

The pol houses of Ahmedabad meet the street over an otla, a plinth wide enough to sit on. The
square in front of them has a chabutra. The agora did the same work for Athens. None of these
were built for conversation. They were built so that people would stop near each other with
no one in charge, and conversation followed. Perspectives took that arrangement off the street
and ran it as a protocol, in cities where the street had mostly stopped doing it. That makes
it urban design by other means.
