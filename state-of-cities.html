About This Work

The State of Cities report was produced during my fellowship at NIUA's Climate Centre for Cities in 2023. As climate fellow, I researched and contributed to this national policy document — covering low-carbon and resilient pathways for Indian cities, published by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

The work involved deep analysis of city-scale energy consumption and GHG emissions data, climate projections, and climate action plans across 15 municipalities — from hill stations like Shimla to industrial cities like Nagpur and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

The report was presented at the G20 Urban20 Summit in Ahmedabad, July 2023.
